# 10-Day Plan: First Paying Client

One offer: an investor-ready financial model in 5 days. PKR 70k or $250, with 50% upfront and 50% on delivery once the client is satisfied.

You spend 1–2 hours a day, around your job process. Claude researches, drafts and sends the emails after your approval. You paste the LinkedIn messages and take the calls.

| Day | Getting customers | Your time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm emails: Zayn VC (asking about portfolio founders), Bridge Factor (subcontract work), Dr. Sahar (referrals). Claude drafts 20 cold emails to founders who recently raised or are raising. | 20 min to approve the emails, 15 min to paste 20 LinkedIn "who do you know" messages |
| 2–5 | 20 founder emails a day, approved in one batch. 20 LinkedIn messages a day. Follow up after 3 days without a reply. | 30–45 min a day |
| 3 | Claude builds the demo SaaS model (.xlsx) to share on calls | 0 |
| 3–7 | Anyone who replies gets a free 3-point review within 24 hours (Claude drafts, you check) | 20 min per review |
| 5–10 | 15-minute calls. Quote PKR 70k and take the 50% deposit through UBL or Raast (USDT for Gulf clients). | Calls |
| 7–10 | Deliver in 5 days. Ask for a referral the day you deliver. | Modelling time |

**Removed from the 20-day version:** the SME dashboard offer, LinkedIn posts, commenting on posts, building a target-list system, and raising prices. None of them lead directly to a first payment.

**Realistic result:** 1–3 deals ($250–750) by day 10. Reaching $1,000 means keeping up the volume through day 30.
