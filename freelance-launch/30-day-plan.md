# 30-Day Launch Plan: Financial Modelling Freelance

## 1. Reality check (read this first)

- **$10,000 in month one from $100 is not a realistic target** for anyone without an audience, a track record, or a product. Treat it as a 6–12 month goal.
- **A realistic month-one target is your first $500–1,500.** That still means 4–6 hours a day of outreach.
- **There is no passive income in month one.** Passive income comes from products that sell without you, and those only sell once you have traffic and proof. Paid client work is how you get both.
- **Payments from Pakistan are a real constraint.** Stripe and PayPal don't pay out to individuals in Pakistan. Gumroad depends on Stripe, so it doesn't work for you. Upwork and Fiverr pay out through Payoneer or a local bank, and that route is tried and tested. Set up Payoneer on day 1.

## 2. Why not PDFs, a fitness app, or "AI consultant"

| Idea | What kills it this month |
|---|---|
| PDFs on Shopify or your own site | Nobody visits a new site. Shopify costs about $39/mo before any sale. Gumroad doesn't pay out to Pakistan. |
| Fitness app | The Apple developer fee ($99/yr) uses up your whole budget. App review takes weeks, the market is crowded, and paid installs need an ad budget. |
| "AI consultant" | Businesses pay consultants for results they've delivered before. A new graduate has none yet, and it's the label everyone is using right now. |
| **Financial models for startups and SMEs** | **You already have the skill and the portfolio (DCF, three-statement model, PE waterfall). Founders need it for fundraising, and it's a real gap: most founders can't build a model and can't afford a Big-4 firm.** |

## 3. The offer

**"Investor-ready financial model in 5 days"**: a fixed scope at a fixed price. Clients buy a clear outcome much more readily than they buy hours.

| Tier | Includes | Launch price | Price after 5 reviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review | Audit and fix the founder's existing model, plus a 1-page list of issues | $80 | $150 |
| Standard | Three-statement model, 36-month forecast, unit economics, use of funds | $250 | $450 |
| Premium | Standard plus scenarios, DCF valuation, cap table and a 1-page investor summary | $500 | $900 |

Second offer: **data analysis for researchers** (Stata, R, Python, regressions, DiD, panel data). Sell analysis and tutoring only. Don't write anyone's thesis.

Launch prices are low on purpose. With zero reviews, you're buying proof. Raise prices after 5 reviews.

## 4. Where to spend the $100

| Item | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork Connects | ~$30–40 | Proposals cost Connects. Check the current price in the app. |
| Fiverr | $0 | Listing is free. Fiverr takes 20% of each sale. |
| LinkedIn outreach | $0 | Direct messages to founders. This converts best and costs nothing. |
| Reserve | ~$60 | Keep it. **Do not run ads.** Ads pointing at a profile with no reviews waste money. |

Sources of leads: seed-stage founders on LinkedIn, startups from Pakistani and Gulf incubators, Upwork posts mentioning "financial model", "pitch deck financials" or "projections", and Reddit's r/startups and r/Entrepreneur (help in the comments, don't spam).

## 5. Daily targets (weeks 1–4)

| Week | Daily | Goal by end of week |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up Payoneer, Upwork and Fiverr. Build one demo model (SaaS startup, 36 months). 10 LinkedIn messages a day. | Profiles live, demo model in portfolio |
| 2 | 8 Upwork proposals, 20 LinkedIn messages | First paid job (any size) |
| 3 | Same, plus deliver fast and ask for a review | 2–3 jobs, first review |
| 4 | Same, plus ask happy clients for referrals | $500–1,500 earned, prices ready to rise |

Track every message in a sheet with these columns: date, name, channel, replied?, call?, paid?. After 2 weeks the numbers will show you which channel works.

## 6. Ready-to-paste copy

> Financial Modeller | Startup Projections, DCF & Investor-Ready Models (Excel)

> Raising money? Investors will ask for your numbers before they ask anything else.
>
> I build clean, investor-ready financial models in Excel. They include three-statement forecasts, unit economics, DCF valuation and cap tables, all built so you can update them yourself.
>
> **Background:** Economics graduate from IBA Karachi (CGPA 3.77, Dean's List), CFA Level I candidate. I've done finance work at Sindh Engro Coal Mining (revenue forecasting) and risk work at United Bank Ltd.
>
> **Examples of my work:** a three-statement valuation of a listed steel company (FCFE/FCFF), a startup model with a PE entry/exit waterfall, and a VaR and credit scorecard model. Links: github.com/maliasghar80-blip
>
> **What you get:** clearly labelled inputs, no hard-coded numbers, a scenario switch, and a short video walkthrough of how to use the model.
>
> Tell me your stage and your raise amount, and I'll tell you what the model needs.

### Fiverr gig title
> I will build an investor-ready financial model and projections for your startup

### Upwork proposal template (edit the first 2 lines for every job)
> Hi [name], you mentioned [specific detail from their post]. The part investors will push on hardest is usually [CAC/payback, burn, or revenue ramp], so I'd build the model around that.
>
> I've built a similar model for a startup, with a DCF and a PE waterfall: [link]. For your case I'd deliver [scope] in [X] days for $[price].
>
> One question so I can quote accurately: [one real question about their business].
>
> Ali

### LinkedIn message to founders (only after connecting; under 60 words)
> Hi [name], congrats on [recent thing: launch, raise or hire]. I build financial models for early-stage founders before a raise (forecasts, unit economics, valuation). If your numbers are still in a rough spreadsheet, I'll review it for free and send you 3 things an investor would flag. Would that be useful?

The free review is your foot in the door. Around 1 in 5 of those reviews should turn into a paid Standard job.

### Follow-up (send after 4 days, once only)
> Hi [name], just following up on this. Happy to send the 3-point review if it would help before your next investor meeting.

## 7. Run a job search in parallel

Freelance income is uneven at first. Keep applying to analyst roles, including remote roles at foreign firms. Your freelance client work also counts as experience on your CV.

## 8. Month 2 onwards: when passive income becomes possible

Once you've done 5 or more client models, you'll know which model buyers want most. Turn it into a template (for example, "SaaS seed-round model") and sell it for $49–99 to your LinkedIn followers and past clients. First confirm that Lemon Squeezy actually pays out to Pakistan by checking their supported-countries page. That template is the "PDF product" idea, built on demand you've already seen instead of a guess.
