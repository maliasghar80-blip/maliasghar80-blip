# Shopify product listing: Fundable Numbers

**Title:** Fundable Numbers

**Subtitle / short description:** Build a startup financial model investors trust. In one weekend.

**Suggested price:** about USD 19 (launch price), with the compare-at price set to about USD 29. The store currency is currently PKR. See the notes below.

**Description (paste into Shopify):**

Investors don't fund spreadsheets. They fund assumptions they believe.

Free templates are everywhere. The hard part is knowing which numbers to put in them, and defending those numbers when an investor pushes back. Fundable Numbers is a 17-page guide that walks you through building a model you can defend, in one weekend.

What's inside:
- The 6 red flags investors spot in founder models, and how to fix each one
- The only 4 tabs you need: inputs, revenue engine, team and costs, cash
- Bottom-up revenue with a full worked SaaS example
- Unit economics that hold up: paid CAC, LTV and payback
- A hiring plan and cost structure that keeps margins honest
- Runway, how much to raise, and use of funds
- Downside, base and upside scenarios with a one-cell switch
- The 10 questions investors ask, and what a strong answer points to
- Tools: an assumption log, an hour-by-hour weekend plan, a 15-point pre-send checklist and a glossary

Format: PDF, 17 pages, A4. Instant download.
For pre-seed and seed founders who can use Excel or Google Sheets but aren't finance people.

Educational content only. Not investment, legal or tax advice.

## Launch checklist (things only you can do)
1. Install the Digital Products app (the link appears in chat).
2. Settings → Payments: connect a gateway. Shopify Payments isn't available in Pakistan. Options are Safepay, PayFast, XPay or UnumPay. Check with them whether they accept **international** cards, because your buyers on X are global.
3. Settings → General → Store currency: consider USD if your gateway supports it. Global buyers won't buy in PKR.
4. Choose a plan when the trial ends, and remove the store password (Online Store → Preferences) so the store is visible to the public.
5. Rename the store from "My Store" to "Fundable Numbers" (Settings → General).
